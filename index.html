STEP 04 — FIGMA DESIGN PROCESS

Empathize & Define

PHASE 01 01
Empathize

Understand the website, audience, and project requirements.

  • Study the existing website, product, and animation script gathered in Steps 02–03.
  • Understand expo audience expectations, brand tone, and the story the animation team is telling.
  • Note which sections rely on production assets (360° and video clips) vs. standard content.
PHASE 02 02
Define

Understand the website structure, define section goals, and identify the required content and assets.

  • Lock the landing page structure identified in Step 03 — Navbar through Footer.
  • Set goals for each section: what it must communicate and which asset feeds it.
  • The sections’ details will change based on the existing website.
07
STEP 04 — FIGMA DESIGN PROCESS

Ideate & Design

PHASE 03 03
Ideate

Explore creative layout ideas, visual arrangements, and alternative section designs to establish an engaging page structure.

  • Explore layout options for the Hero, Products, and video-driven sections.
  • Brainstorm how the 360° view and video clips sit visually within the page flow.
  • Sketch quick alternatives for sections that vary by source website.
PHASE 04 04
Design

Create high-fidelity Figma designs with brand styling, typography, imagery, and media assets.

  • Build the high-fidelity mockup directly in Figma, following the Step 03 order.
  • Apply brand styling, imagery, color theory, typography and copy across every section.
  • Place placeholders for animation-team assets (360°, Video Clips 01 & 02).
08
STEP 04 — FIGMA DESIGN PROCESS

Prototype & Test

PHASE 05 05
Prototype

Explore creative layout ideas, visual arrangements, and alternative section designs to establish an engaging page structure.

  • Link the Figma frames into an interactive, clickable prototype.
  • Simulate scroll behavior and embedded video / 360° interaction points.
  • Preview navbar links and in-page navigation
PHASE 06 06
Test

Create high-fidelity Figma designs with brand styling, typography, imagery, and media assets.

  • Walk through the prototype internally against the Step 03 checklist.
  • Meet Client’s/website needs and confirm animation assets are correctly placed.
  • Iterate the Figma design until it is ready for QC or Self analysis by designers.
